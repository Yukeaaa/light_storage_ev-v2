## 权利要求（逐字）

1. A method comprising using at least one hardware processor to: generate an optimization model for at least one power infrastructure site, wherein the at least one power infrastructure site comprises one or more power infrastructure assets, wherein at least one of the one or more power infrastructure assets is associated with an energy-reserve profile that decreases energy available to that power infrastructure asset over time, and wherein the optimization model outputs a target value based on one or more variables, which represent an operating configuration of the at least one power infrastructure site, while constrained by the energy-reserve profile associated with the at least one power infrastructure asset; solve the optimization model to determine values for the one or more variables that optimize the target value; and initiate control of one or more physical components in the at least one power infrastructure site based on the determined values for the one or more variables.

2. The method of Claim 1, wherein the optimization model is generated and solved periodically to determine the one or more variables that represent the operating configuration of the at least one power infrastructure site in a sliding time window that slides according to a time step.

3. The method of Claim 2, wherein the energy-reserve profile associated with the at least one power infrastructure asset slides with the sliding time window according to the time step.

4. The method of Claim 2, wherein the energy-reserve profile associated with the at least one power infrastructure asset is anchored to a scheduled event.

5. The method of Claim 4, wherein the at least one power infrastructure site is a depot, wherein the at least one power infrastructure asset is an electric vehicle, and wherein the scheduled event is an arrival or departure of the electric vehicle at the depot.

## 说明书（Definitions 段）中与"电网限功率"相关的段落（**注意：不在权项内**）

> "Such disturbances include, without limitation, electric vehicles returning and being plugged in late,
> electric vehicles returning with a lower state of charge than planned, ..., **curtailment of power demand
> limits by the operator of the power grid**, unexpected reductions in on-site power generation ..."

> "Such disturbances can reduce the time and/or power available to charge the electric vehicles, which may
> result in electric vehicles not being ready for departure at their scheduled departure times."

> "To prepare electric vehicles by business-critical deadlines in a reliable manner, the control system
> must be capable of anticipating such uncertainties."

> "an energy-reserve profile decreases the energy available to the associated power infrastructure asset over time."
> "such a constraint reserves energy for the power infrastructure asset to mitigate the uncertainty of the future.
> The further into the future that charging ... is scheduled, the more energy that is reserved ..."
