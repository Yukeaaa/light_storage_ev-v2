Driven by the goals of carbon peak and carbon neutrality, the new power system has developed rapidly, exhibiting main
characteristics such as strong stochastic volatility, high power electronization, and significant susceptibility to external influences,
whichposenumerouschallengestothesecure,stable,andeconomicoperationofpowergrids.Simultaneously,theswiftadvancement
ofartificialintelligencehasenabledittoinitiallypossessthecapabilitiesofautonomousknowledgediscoveryandinference,thereby
drivingatransformationofthescientificresearchparadigmfromtraditionalmechanism-andexperiment-drivenapproachestodata-
and computing-driven ones, which provides a new path to address the issues in the new power system. From a system-level
perspective, this paper focuses on artificial intelligence technologies tailored for the new power system, summarizing the current
development status and emerging trends in core application areas, including simulation analysis, planning, dispatch and operation,
stability control, and electricity market transactions. Furthermore, it discusses several technical bottlenecks and key challenges
associatedwith the artificialintelligence-assisted newpower system.
