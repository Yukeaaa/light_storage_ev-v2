and charging power stations, and then analyzes in detail the communication architecture, energy management system, and microgrid
controller functions of the charging station. By coordinating and optimizing the operation of photovoltaic, energy storage, and charging
piles in the station, the goal of energy conservation, carbon reduction, emission reduction, and economic benefits can be achieved.
