压力、提升能源利用效率提供了新路径。文章选择某光储充一体化项目，探讨其充电桩的能源智能协

该能源智能协同机制具有提升能源利用效率、改善电网负荷特性等作用，为光储充一体化充电桩的推

Research on energy intelligent coordination mechanism of photovoltaic-
storage-charging integrated charging piles
MA Cheng LU Haiman ZHONG Hongming
Abstract： As an innovative solution integrating renewable energy generation, energy storage, and smart
charging, the photovoltaic-storage-charging (PV-storage-charging) integrated technology provides a new
pathway to alleviate grid pressure and improve energy utilization efficiency. This article selects a certain PV-
storage-charging integrated project to explore the energy intelligent coordination mechanism of its charging
stations. By analyzing the system composition and main components of the project, an energy intelligent
coordination mechanism for the project was established. The study shows that this mechanism can improve
energy utilization efficiency and enhance grid load characteristics, providing a reference for the promotion and
application of PV-storage-charging integrated charging stations.
Keywords: photovoltaic-storage-charging integrated; charging piles; energy smart coordination; smart grid
