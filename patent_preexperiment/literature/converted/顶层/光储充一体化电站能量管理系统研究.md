Abstract: This paper proposes an optimized scheduling and management strategy for photovoltaic-storage charging
stations involving multiple stakeholders. The research emphasizes the interaction and collaborative scheduling among photo-
voltaic systems, energy storage units, EV charging infrastructure, and the power grid within the station. By designing an ef-
ficient software architecture, a layered management system is developed, encompassing data acquisition, transmission, pro-
cessing, decision-making, control, user interaction, and visualization layers. This system enhances the energy flow and
charging schedules of the photovoltaic-storage charging station. Real-time analysis of the system’s operational state is
conducted at the decision-making layer to ensure its operational efficiency and stability.
